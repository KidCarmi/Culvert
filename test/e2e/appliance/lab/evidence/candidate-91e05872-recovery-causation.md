## Reading it

1. **The esxi12 profile injects a fixed delay** (`R-esxi12-dm-table.txt`: `delay 7:0 0 12 7:0 0 3` —
   12 ms per read, 3 ms per write). On the A/B runner the device served reads at **12.04–12.08 ms**, i.e. the
   injected delay and essentially nothing else. On run 37966305990's runner the SAME reads took
   **14.9–18.0 ms** and writes 4.1–8.5 ms: 3–6 ms per request came from the runner's own storage.
2. **The slowdown is present in a phase with no Culvert code.** restart → first kernel line (firmware,
   bootloader, kernel+initrd read) is 56–61 s vs 44.6–45.6 s. Nothing the candidate does runs there.
3. **It accounts for the whole excess.** Against the mean of the six A runs on the A/B runner:

| reboot | recovery delta | device busy-time (io_ticks) delta |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | +27.8 s | +27.6 s |
| 2 | +19.4 s | +19.7 s |
| 3 | +33.6 s | +38.4 s |

   Recovery grew by the time the device spent on the same requests, within 5 s on every reboot.
4. **The candidate itself is stable and within budget.** Twelve reboots on one runner, two candidates, A B B A:
   104.8–107.2 s; 91e05872 vs dc57bd76 indistinguishable. ASTRA's own ESXi measurement of 91e05872:
   74.1 / 69.4 / 69.4 s.

## What this does NOT explain (still separate items)

- The historical ESXi readings of 193 / 336 / 463 / 497 / 597 s on earlier candidates, and the backup-listing
  timeout. Those predate this candidate, were on ESXi (not this QEMU profile), and are not attributed here.
- Which property of the shared runner made its disk slower. The attribution is to host device service time,
  measured; the runner's underlying cause is outside what the job can observe.

## Consequence for qualification

A QEMU recovery number is only comparable with another taken under the same device service time. The
qualifying protocol stays: one runner, interleaved A/B, and the per-request service time recorded beside every
reading (it is, in `host_device`). A single-run reading above budget with service time above the injected
delay is environment, not a regression; one with service time AT the injected delay is a regression.
